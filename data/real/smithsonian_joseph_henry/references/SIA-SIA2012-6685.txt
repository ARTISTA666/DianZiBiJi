Arranged needles on the outside of the two poles as in the diagram, found them all magnetized minus. This is an instructive experiment from which it would appear that the lines of current are similar to those on the surface of paper on which filings have been strewed above a magnet.
Refer in the account of these experiments to those of Professor Darrell on the divergency of a galvanic current.
